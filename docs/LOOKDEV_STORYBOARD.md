# Look Development and Storyboard

## Lookboard

Purpose: choose how the project looks.

Show a small set of high-value representative images:
- main character(s)
- main location(s)
- day/night or lighting system
- signature prop(s)
- one or two spectacle/emotion moments

References should carry visual information directly. Avoid long style paraphrases.

## Storyboard

Purpose: preview how the story plays.

Use it to evaluate:
- story clarity
- compositions
- staging
- reveals
- approximate shot rhythm
- transitions between major beats

Do not treat one storyboard sheet as a precision production keyframe.

### X

Storyboard may use the selected X visual language: photography, realistic illustration, or realistic comic.

### Y

Storyboard must be live-action / photorealistic cinematic. No comic look and no 3D-animation look.

For Seedance production, generate separate high-fidelity keyframes after storyboard approval.
